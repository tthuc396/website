Exotic
High Movement

Exotic Collection

The family for dramatic veining, stronger focal slabs, and rooms where the surface should do more visual work.

  • Representative names include Seren Blue, Fantasy Gold, Rocky Gold, Luxe Blue, and Sahara Gold.
  • Best for hospitality features, statement islands, and applications where the slab carries the room.
  • Available across the same Jumbo and Super Jumbo structure used throughout the source catalog.
View Exotic
Natural
Soft Veining

Natural Collection

The calmer family for brighter kitchens, baths, and hospitality interiors that need cleaner Carrara or Bianca-style movement.

  • Representative names include Carrara Abraba, Carrara Venatino, Carrara Alto Gold, and Bianca Gold.
  • Best for lighter premium rooms where the surface should stay balanced with millwork and furniture.
  • Use this family when the project wants elegance without high visual drama.
View Natural
Grain
Uniform Texture

Grain Collection

The practical family for steadier granular and concrete-like surfaces where consistency matters more than dramatic veining.

  • Representative names include Pepper White, Pure White, Cemento, Smoky Gray, Latte, and Pure Concrete.
  • Best for support spaces, repeated room conditions, and projects that want more controlled pattern behavior.
  • Use Grain when durability and repeatability matter more than statement-surface presence.
