How It Gets Reviewed

What helps us respond faster.

You do not need a full FF&E book to start. A clear room brief is usually enough for the first pass.

What to send

Share the restaurant type, floor plan, seat count, target opening, finish direction, inspiration images, and any brand standards already in hand.

What we confirm first

We confirm whether the job fits a simple furniture review, custom development, or a mixed approach tied to the cabinet and countertop package.

Where design help fits

Use design help when furniture needs to coordinate with cabinetry, slab choices, brand mood, or built-in millwork before production notes are finalized.
