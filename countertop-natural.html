Natural

The lighter Carrara and Bianca side of the slab catalog.

Natural is the calmer quartz family for brighter kitchens, baths, and hospitality rooms that need elegance without dramatic, high-contrast veining.

Quote Natural
Visual behavior

Soft veining, cleaner white fields, and calmer premium movement.

Slab sizes

Jumbo slab 126 x 63 inches and Super Jumbo 3500 x 2000mm.

Thickness

20mm (0.78 inch) on the published collection pages.

Material specs

Natural keeps the same quartz performance with a brighter slab field.

Use the family when the room needs a lighter stone read without moving into overly dramatic veining.

Material

Asina Global quartz is made with more than 90% natural quartz, creating a hard surface with a polished or matte-ready finish and very little routine maintenance.

Performance

Quartz rates about 7 on the Mohs scale, giving the slab strong scratch, chip, stain, and everyday wear resistance across kitchen and bath use.

Care

Low porosity helps block water penetration and supports an easy-clean, anti-bacterial surface that also handles short hot-pot or boiling-water contact.
