Exotic

The boldest veining and strongest statement-surface movement.

Asina Global Exotic Collection layers large flowing veins across light and dark quartz bases, giving statement islands, feature counters, and high-visibility rooms a more dramatic natural-stone read.

Quote Exotic
Product surface

Polish finish for a brighter reflective slab face.

Standard size

126 x 63 inches (3200 x 1600mm) and 137 x 78 inches (3500 x 2000mm).

Thickness

20mm (0.78 inch) and 30mm (1.18 inch).

Material specs

Asina Global Exotic pairs large-vein quartz visuals with production-ready slab specs.

Use this family when you want a more dramatic natural look, but still need dependable quartz sizing, weight ranges, and fabrication planning.

Material

Asina Global quartz is made with more than 90% natural quartz, giving the slab a hard surface with low maintenance and a more refined finish direction.

Weight

About 51kg per square meter (112.4 lbs) at 20mm and 70kg per square meter (154.3 lbs) at 30mm.

Performance

Quartz rates about 7 on the Mohs scale, with strong resistance to scratching, chipping, staining, heat exposure, and water penetration.
