Project fit

What kinds of projects fit, what to send first, and how multiple categories can move together.

What types of projects do you support?

We focus on residential multifamily build-outs, hospitality work, restaurant build-outs, and other projects with enough scope to support the supply model. The business is based in Central Florida and can coordinate qualifying projects nationwide.

Is this only for larger commercial work?

No. Residential projects also fit when the scope aligns with the collections and the container-based supply model.

Do you only serve Florida?

No. Asina Global is based in Central Florida and supports local project work directly, but qualifying project work can also be quoted and coordinated nationwide.

Do I need to start with one category?

Start with the main product category first, then add cabinets, countertops, or furniture scope together if the package needs it.

Can one project include cabinets, countertops, and furniture together?

Yes. We can coordinate the full package and work with you to fill one container with the products your project needs.

What should I send to start a quote?

Send the product category, project type, location, timeline, and any plans, dimensions, elevations, inspiration images, or scope notes you already have.

Do I need drawings before reaching out?

Drawings are not required, but they help us understand exactly what you want. Accurate quoting is harder without drawings, dimensions, or clear scope information.

Custom + design help

When to use design help, where custom work fits, and how finish coordination is handled.

Do you support custom work?

Yes. Custom sizing, finish coordination, and room-specific guidance can be discussed when the standard catalog is not enough.

Do you offer custom sizing or custom specifications?

Yes. For cabinets, we can cut custom. For furniture, we can review chairs, table bases, benches, and custom restaurant pieces after the specifications are reviewed during consultation.

When should I use design help instead of just requesting a quote?

Use design help when the room needs finish matching, custom details, layout guidance, or clearer notes for production.

Do you help choose finishes and materials?

Yes. Our in-house design team can help align cabinet, surface, restaurant furniture, and finish choices across the room.

Do you show furniture options online?

No. The public site keeps restaurant furniture intentionally general. Contact us for chairs, table bases, benches, and franchise or theme-driven restaurant furniture.

How does the process usually work?

Most projects move through review and final decisions first, then manufacturing, then shipping so the approved details reach production clearly.

Timeline + shipping

Lead times, container shipping, Incoterms responsibilities, moisture protection, and how freight cost is handled in the quote.

How long does consultation and design finalization take?

Consultation and design finalization usually takes about 3 weeks, depending on how quickly selections, drawings, and approvals are completed.

How long does manufacturing take?

Manufacturing typically takes about 40 to 50 days, depending on current factory load and order complexity.

How long does shipping take?

Transit times are typically 22 to 30 days to the West Coast and 40 to 50 days to the East Coast under DAP (Delivered at Place). Orders usually move in 20-foot or 40-foot containers.

How much fits in a container?

A 40HC container typically fits about 700 to 800 cabinet boxes, while a 20H container typically fits about 370 boxes. Final capacity depends on the mix of sizes and product types in the order.

What are Incoterms?

Incoterms (International Commercial Terms) define the responsibilities of buyers and sellers in international trade. There are 11 official terms published by the International Chamber of Commerce. For Sea and Inland Waterway Transport Only, the terms are FAS, FOB, CFR, and CIF.

Can lead times change?

Yes. Timelines can change based on factory load, production scheduling, shipping conditions, and how quickly project approvals are finalized.

How is shipping cost handled?

Shipping cost is quoted according to the agreed Incoterm and destination. Final responsibility for freight, insurance, terminal handling, customs, and onward delivery depends on the trade term selected for the order, so we confirm those details again when the order is placed.

How do you prevent moisture during shipping?

We place continuous lines of desiccant bags or silica gel packets along both sides of the container to absorb humidity and reduce condensation, mold, and moisture damage in transit.

Cabinet material FAQ

What changes by collection?

Face materials shift by line: Malibu uses painted shaker faces, Monterey uses stained wood options, Newport uses melamine on MDF, Catalina uses lacquer finish on MDF, Laguna uses soft-touch matte, and Jersey keeps a white shaker look on the frameless platform.

What stays consistent?

The construction story stays practical: collection-specific fronts, plywood box systems, and material notes that help you compare before quoting.

Why this matters before quoting

Knowing the face material, box build, and door style early makes it easier to compare collections, align budget, and send the right package into production.
