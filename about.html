How the process works

01 / Start with the catalog

Open the cabinet, countertop, or furniture catalog first and narrow the collection and finish.

02 / Add project context

Share the room type, scope, location, timeline, and any plans, elevations, or reference images you already have.

03 / Bring in design help when needed

Use the in-house senior design team when the room needs custom details, tighter finish coordination, or clearer notes for production.
