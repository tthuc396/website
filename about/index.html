About Asina FAQ

Who is Asina built for?

Builders, developers, procurement teams, general contractors, restaurant groups, franchise operators, hospitality teams, and rollout buyers with project-scale or repeat-project needs.

Is Asina a retail remodeling catalog?

No. Asina shows cabinet, countertop, and furniture package options so qualified buyers can prepare a project review, not shop a cart.

Does Asina disclose factories or production sources?

No. Asina acts as supplier of record and does not disclose private production sources, factory identities, or supplier relationships.

Can one project include multiple categories?

Yes. Cabinets, countertops, and custom furniture packages can be reviewed together when the scope benefits from one coordinated supply review.

What happens after project basics are sent?

Asina reviews the information and follows up by email within 1-2 business days to request drawings or specs if the project fits.

Where is Asina based?

Asina is based in Longwood, Florida and can coordinate qualified project work nationwide.
