Kitchen Trends

10 quartz countertop directions shaping modern kitchens.

These are actual slab directions tied to GSV codes, built around the palettes builders and developers ask for most often.

Browse Countertops

01 / Calm Whites

Use controlled white slabs when the room already has enough detail.

  • GSV-7101 Carrara White
  • GSV-1101 Pure White
  • GSV-1103 Super White

02 / Warm Veining

Gold and taupe movement keeps a modern kitchen from feeling cold.

  • GSV-8101 Carrara Alto Gold
  • GSV-8113 Avenza Brown
  • GSV-9176 Sahara Gold

03 / Statement Islands

Save the strongest slab for the island when you want a focal surface.

  • GSV-9205 Alaska Blue
  • GSV-9174 Fantasy Gold
  • GSV-9132 Calacatta Stella

04 / Dark Contrast

Black and deep-grey slabs sharpen a cleaner kitchen palette.

  • GSV-9114 Calacatta Storm Black
  • GSV-9134 Nero Marquina
  • GSV-1105 Cemento

05 / How To Choose

Start with the family, then narrow the slab.

Use Natural for calmer premium rooms, Exotic for stronger visual movement, and Grain for repeatable cleaner texture. That sequence works especially well for projects balancing bright light, open plans, and warmer finish palettes.

Build the shortlist.

Send the GSV codes that fit the kitchen direction and we can narrow the quote around slab family, quantity, edge profile, and cutouts.
