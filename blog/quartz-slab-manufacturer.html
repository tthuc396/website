Manufacturer Fit

What to look for in a quartz slab manufacturer before you buy.

For Orlando and Central Florida projects, the right source is not just a slab library. You need consistency, size clarity, documented factory systems, and real project support.

Request Countertop Quote

01 / Material

Start with composition and consistency.

You want a dense engineered quartz surface with stable finish quality and dependable pattern control across repeat orders.

02 / Slab Format

Sizes and thicknesses should be clear up front.

Asina Global quartz programs are organized around 3200 x 1600mm and 3500 x 2000mm slabs, with 20mm and 30mm options depending on collection. That clarity matters when islands, vanities, and repeated room types are being priced together.

03 / Standards

Documented sourcing beats vague claims.

For our quartz programs, we position the line around NSF slabs and ISO 9001-managed factory systems. That is the level of baseline builders and developers should expect when evaluating a source partner.

04 / Support

The supplier should help close the spec.

One of Asina Global's biggest advantages is designer coordination. Our in-house team can translate the interior direction into cabinet, countertop, and furniture decisions so Orlando and Central Florida projects move as one package.

05 / Quote Inputs

Better quotes start with cleaner scope.

  • Collection family or GSV shortlist
  • Estimated square footage or quantity
  • Edge profile and cutout requirements
  • Whether the project also includes cabinets, furniture, or designer support
