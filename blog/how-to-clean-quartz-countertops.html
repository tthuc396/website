Quartz Care

How to clean quartz countertops without dulling the finish.

For Orlando and Central Florida kitchens, the right routine keeps quartz clear, bright, and easier to live with over time.

Ask About Quartz

01 / Daily Clean

Use a soft cloth, warm water, and a pH-neutral cleaner.

Most day-to-day cleanup does not need anything aggressive. A microfiber cloth or soft sponge will remove normal spills and residue without stressing the polished face.

02 / Avoid

Skip abrasives and strong chemicals.

Do not use wire brushes, steel wool, harsh scouring pads, or heavy solvents. Quartz is durable, but repeated abrasion and aggressive chemicals can wear the resin layer that gives the slab its finished surface.

03 / Protect

Heat and direct cutting still need to be managed.

  • Use trivets or heat pads for hot cookware.
  • Use cutting boards instead of cutting directly on the slab.
  • Wipe spills early so you never need to scrub harder than necessary.

04 / Placement

Be careful with prolonged direct sun.

Indoor quartz performs well, but very long UV exposure in bright sunrooms or window walls can shift color over time. In Orlando and across Central Florida, that matters more in rooms with strong afternoon sun.

05 / Collections

The same routine applies across Exotic, Natural, and Grain.

The care baseline stays consistent even when the slab mood changes. Good habits matter just as much on a quiet white kitchen as they do on a more dramatic statement island.
