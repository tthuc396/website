Construction, finish and installation notes

Built to stay square for years

  • Commercial-grade materials and hardware come standard.
  • Thicker panels, stronger joints, smoother function.

Stronger box

  • Stays square: reinforced with Confirmat screws and dowels.
  • Resists warping: thick panels and solid back support.

Cleaner finish

  • Tighter reveals: precise panel alignment for consistent gaps.
  • Finished interiors are described as cleaner looking and longer lasting.

Easier install

  • The 3/4-inch back lets uppers mount directly to the wall; no rails required.
  • Commercial adjustable legs support faster leveling.
  • Front and box adjustments support a dialed-in fit.

    Set the direction

    Choose room style, door profile, finish, Quick Book references, accessories and countertop direction.

    Review and customize

    Approved specifications guide production review, QA, packing and project-specific shipping planning.
