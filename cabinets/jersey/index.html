Jersey Cabinet FAQ

What is Jersey best for?

Jersey can be reviewed for project-scale supply when finish direction, construction facts, and quantity are ready for pricing review.

What should buyers send?

Send finish choice, room type, cabinet run, unit count, location, timeline, and any known approval or packing needs.

Does Asina disclose production sources?

No. Asina acts as supplier of record and protects private source relationships while managing the project review.
