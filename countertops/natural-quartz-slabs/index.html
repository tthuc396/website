Natural Quartz FAQ

What is Natural best for?

Natural is best for projects that want a quartz surface with a softer natural-stone read and practical project facts.

What should buyers send?

Send slab code, square footage, edge profile, cutouts, destination, timeline, and whether cabinets are part of the scope.

Can Natural slabs fit commercial rooms?

Yes. Asina reviews use case, square footage, edge needs, packing, and shipping responsibility before pricing.
